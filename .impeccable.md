## Design Context

### Users
Independent investors and equity researchers reviewing public SEC insider buying data, on desktop for deeper research and on phones for quick checks. They need to find clustered purchases, understand who bought and how much, verify filings, and manage alerts.

### Brand Personality
Measured, discerning, transparent. Build confidence through legible data and source attribution, with no promises about returns.

### Aesthetic Direction
A financial research publication: warm paper, forest green, restrained ochre, editorial serif headings paired with a clean sans serif. Spacious entry points and efficient research surfaces. The user confirmed this direction for the revamp.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with the evidence; clearly distinguish live data from illustrative examples.
2. Use strong typographic hierarchy and deliberate whitespace rather than decoration.
3. Preserve all research functions on mobile; use readable stacked records for wide tables.
4. Make navigation, focus states, and touch targets usable with keyboard and touch.
5. Keep public content server rendered, source linked, and reachable through existing canonical URLs.
