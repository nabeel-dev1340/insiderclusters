# InsiderClusters SEO audit

Audit date: October 13, 2026. Target: https://insiderclusters.com. Keyword market: Google, United States, English.

Follow-up: indexing exports were subsequently supplied and the first implementation batch is documented in [SEO-IMPLEMENTATION-2026-10-13.md](SEO-IMPLEMENTATION-2026-10-13.md). Findings below record the original audit snapshot.

## Assessment

The site has working crawl fundamentals and useful original filing data, but very little demonstrated traffic for its core search terms. The priorities are to investigate the late-September visibility decline, make the stock directory substantially smaller, focus existing educational pages on measured demand, and earn relevant editorial links.

The 200-page DataForSEO crawl scored 98.14/100. All 200 sampled URLs returned 200, and the crawl found no duplicate titles, duplicate descriptions, or broken internal links. This is a technical checklist score, not a measure of ranking strength. The sample contained 182 stock pages and only two insider profiles, so it does not establish sitewide health.

## On-page and content findings

**P2: shorten generated titles.** DataForSEO flags 190 of the 200 sampled titles as too long. This largely reflects the stock-heavy sample. The NVCT title is 93 characters: `NVCT Insider Buying — Cluster Buys & Form 4 History | Nuvectis Pharma, Inc. | InsiderClusters`. An example replacement is `NVCT Insider Buying & Form 4 History | InsiderClusters`, with the company name prominent in the page content. Many descriptions are also approximately 200 characters. Lead with the ticker, purchase history, and specific useful detail; avoid long repeated boilerplate. Google's display depends on available width, not a strict character rule. See [Google's title guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link).

**P2: give `/stocks` a clear current-research purpose.** Its GSC CTR is extremely low, but the query mix is largely hidden. Inspect its own page-filtered GSC queries before treating a title rewrite as the whole solution. A smaller directory can surface recent purchases, filing dates, and clear source links first, then link to historical data. Distinguish transaction date, filing date, and detection date. Do not imply that an old cluster happened today.

**P2: improve existing learning pages before expanding page counts.** The transaction-code guide already exists and has 17 GSC impressions at an average position of 5.18, but zero clicks. This is a small sample, not an established ranking. Add a concise answer, complete code table, real filing examples, source links, and relevant links into stock research. Apply similar improvements to the 10b5-1 and filing-deadline guides. Use genuine author/reviewer identities and qualifications where available; never invent financial credentials.

**P3: add discovery paths for deeper insider profiles.** `/insiders` displays a top-100 leaderboard, while the insider sitemap has 9,048 candidates. Relevant ticker and archive pages provide additional links, so this is not a finding that every remaining profile is orphaned. Consider paginated insider browsing and contextual links from related records. Evaluate weak profiles using indexing evidence and their actual unique data before changing eligibility rules.

The site's responses contain Organization, BreadcrumbList, Dataset, Article, ProfilePage, and FAQPage JSON-LD on appropriate sampled templates. Source presence was verified; browser-rendered schema and Google's rich-result validation were not completed because no browser was available. Do not interpret DataForSEO's static `has_micromarkup: false` flag as missing JSON-LD. Schema should describe the visible content accurately; additional FAQ markup is not a demonstrated ranking fix.

DataForSEO's low text-to-HTML flags are also not sufficient evidence of thin content: React scripts, serialization, and tables inflate markup. Evaluate the useful visible data and explanations rather than adding filler to improve that ratio.

## Authority and links

DataForSEO reports three backlinks from two referring domains, `seonix.agency` and `elitetrade.club`. This is limited detected coverage, not a complete backlink inventory. It suggests little established external authority compared with the domains dominating the sampled SERPs.

Prioritize original, reproducible research that finance writers and newsletters can cite: a monthly cluster summary, transparent methodology, dated examples, and links to underlying filings. Keep interpretation bounded by the data. Pursue relevant editorial references over bulk directory links. No outreach has been sent, and no disavow action is recommended from these two domains alone.

## Action plan

1. Obtain GSC Page indexing, Sitemaps, and Crawl stats exports. Inspect `/stocks`, one current profile, one historical 404 profile, one ticker page, and the code guide. Determine the timing and extent of the visibility decline before claiming a cause.
2. Paginate `/stocks` and the largest sector lists with crawlable links, self-canonicals, and full-dataset search. Verify response size, mobile performance, and access to deep records.
3. Consolidate www with a permanent redirect. Shorten ticker/profile/article titles and oversized descriptions while retaining the existing URLs.
4. Improve the transaction-code, 10b5-1, deadline, and filing-walkthrough articles. Link them to relevant research pages and make dates and sources explicit.
5. Publish useful recurring research and earn relevant citations. Measure intended-query impressions, clicks, sign-ups, and subscriptions by page family over 28-day windows. Record deployment dates and avoid drawing conclusions from a handful of clicks.

No changes to SEO implementation or production configuration were made during this audit. Earlier support-email edits remain in the working tree.
